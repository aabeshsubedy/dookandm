Scope

What DokaanDM doesn’t do

What a tool refuses to build tells you more than its feature list. Every one of these is a deliberate choice, not a gap we plan to quietly fill.

  • No WhatsApp or TikTok

    Facebook and Instagram share one messaging platform, so we can do both properly. Adding a third channel before those two are genuinely good would make all of them worse.

  • No AI auto-replies

    Your customers came to talk to you. COD risk is plain arithmetic on your own delivery history, so you can read the rule and disagree with it.

  • No payment processing

    We never touch your money, which is exactly why we never take a cut of it. Orders record the payment type and reference; collecting the cash stays between you and your buyer.

  • No storefront to maintain

    There is no shop page to design, host, or drive traffic to. You already have an audience in your DMs; this is the software behind it, not another website.
